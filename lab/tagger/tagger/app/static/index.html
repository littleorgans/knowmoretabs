Tags in view

      Local prototype on 127.0.0.1. Tags made here stay in the data folder on this machine until you export them; the archive is never written.

      Keyboard

      /
      Search.
      space x
      Select the focused page, or unselect it. A click does the same.
      o
      Open the focused page in a new tab. Selects nothing.
      f
      Forget the focused page: it leaves every view. Selects nothing.
      p
      Pin the focused page for later, or unpin it. Selects nothing.
      u Ctrl Z
      Undo, while its message shows.
      Esc
      Leave the ≈ or Pinned view; with none open, clear the selection.
      e
      Export.
      t
      Light or dark.
      ?
      This help.

      In the tag strip, a tag's name adds it to the selected pages, its × takes it off them, its count is how many of them have it, and ≈ finds similar pages without that tag. A tag's × on a page takes it off that page only.
